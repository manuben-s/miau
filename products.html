To be honest, I hate that nowadays everything is a product. We are constantly trying to monetize everything, sometimes thinking about that first and about the process of learning afterwards. So fuck that.
But I need money.

Nah, out of seriousness.

Every now and then, after exploring a few ideas, I come across things that I think are worth bringing out into the world as objects. You'll find here some of them.

I care a lot about the process behind everything I do, so I’ll always try to keep things fair in terms of quality, materials, people and the environment, and make things by hand whenever I can. If you think I’m missing something, please reach out.

I also think it's lovely to have pieces made around your specific taste and needs. So instead of an "Add to cart" button, you'll find a contact form. You'll see a price range and the customisation options for each product. If you have an idea outside those options, don't be shy. It might be fun for me to explore it.

Also, try to be honest with yourself and with me. I'll set a minimum price for each product, keeping in mind that not everyone has the same possibilities (yup, I was also once a student struggling to have enough money to support the things I wanted to do… well, technically, I still am). But if you feel like you want and can afford to give more, that support helps me keep making things, exploring ideas and bringing them into the world. It's completely up to you.

Once you get in touch, I'll tell you if I think the idea is realistic, how much time it would take, and the final price.

Looking forward to hearing from you.

Cheeeeeeeeeeeeers!
